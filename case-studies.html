ND Stars Beauty Salon

East London's first dedicated laser hair removal clinic was growing fast — but their digital infrastructure hadn't kept up. They had no website worth mentioning, no consistent way to capture enquiries out of hours, and were manually answering calls and booking appointments between treatments.

The result: missed calls, inconsistent client experience, and staff spending valuable treatment time on admin that could be automated.

What We Built

  • A conversion-focused website with full treatment information, pricing clarity, and social proof
  • 24/7 AI voice receptionist trained on their specific services, FAQs, and booking process
  • Direct calendar integration — the AI books appointments in real time, not just takes messages
  • Post-call SMS confirmations and reminders to reduce no-shows

The Challenge

A beauty clinic's busiest enquiry times are often evenings and weekends — exactly when no one is available to answer the phone. Before INSAT, an 8pm enquiry on a Friday would wait until Monday for a response. By then, most callers had already booked elsewhere.

HR Managing

HR Managing is a UK SaaS platform helping employers stay compliant with Home Office immigration requirements, specifically around Sponsor Licence management. Their product is genuinely useful — but their existing web presence didn't communicate that.

They needed a marketing site that could translate a complex regulatory product into something a non-expert business owner would understand, trust, and buy. They also had a specific requirement: a live tool that let businesses check their Sponsor Licence status in real time.

What We Built

  • Complete marketing website with conversion-focused landing pages for each product feature
  • A live Sponsor Licence Checker tool that queries real-time UK government data
  • Design system built for long-term scalability — new features can be added without redesign
  • SEO architecture targeting specific compliance and HR keywords in competitive UK market

The Challenge

Most HR compliance products either over-explain (drowning the visitor in legal jargon) or under-explain (leaving them uncertain what they're actually buying). The copywriting brief was: make someone who has never thought about Sponsor Licences understand why they need one, what happens if they don't have the right processes, and why HR Managing is the specific solution to that problem.

The Sponsor Licence Checker required a backend integration to pull live UKVI register data and present it in a format that was immediately useful to a non-technical business owner.
